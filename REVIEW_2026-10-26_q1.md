## 3. Verification performed

- Every new headline AUROC was recomputed from the saved prediction files with scikit-learn and matched the generated tables.
- DeLong was checked against a recording bootstrap (intervals 0.015–0.060 vs 0.014–0.059).
- The d1-3B full-set merge was checked against the stored subset run (max difference 3e-8).
- HuBERT-ECG authors' preprocessing was checked against a literal re-implementation (6e-8).
- Seed blocks and recalibration draws were checked to be complete.
- Unit tests: 11 OK.
- Claims in the text were checked against `paired_comparisons.csv`. Wording was corrected where it overstated:
  - SQI PPV range;
  - CinC 2017 comparisons;
  - n = 200 sensitivity range;
  - "outperformed";
  - demographics.
