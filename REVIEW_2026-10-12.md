## 3. Verification performed

- The OOF-based cohort summary was reproduced exactly from the stored predictions before regeneration (all 8 models). After regeneration the summary is produced by `evaluation/build_manuscript_tables.py` from the new predictions, with the same bootstrap function.
- Tables 1b and 2b operating points were reproduced from the predictions with the stated tie-break rule (before regeneration; the same rule is used in the builder).

- Parameter counts were recomputed from the model definitions.

- Seed stability (section 4.5): BUT QDB pooled AUROC varies by at most 0.02 across seeds for each CNN/multimodal model; CinC AUROC of the CNNs varies by at most 0.02 (1D 0.710–0.726, 2D 0.746–0.766); multimodal net outputs are constant for seeds 1–3 on CinC.

## 4. Scientific conclusions that changed

These differ from the earlier manuscript and are the main reason the manuscript was rewritten.

1. **Multimodal fusion gives no benefit in-domain or in transfer.** BUT QDB AUROC 0.967 (unimodal CNNs 0.989). On CinC the multimodal net gave AUROC 0.627 for seed 42 with two distinct output values, and constant outputs for three of four additional seeds. The earlier claim that fusion transferred better (0.763) does not hold after the corrections.
2. **The 2D CNN's default-threshold sensitivity on CinC depends on the seed**: 17.3% for seed 42, 40–43% for seeds 1–4. The earlier narrative based on seed 42 alone is replaced.
3. **The tabular transfer failure is not only a scaling artifact.** Near-constant saturation and flatline features in BUT QDB inflate z-scores on CinC, and several SQIs reverse their association across databases (high-frequency noise ratio: AUROC 0.777 in BUT QDB vs 0.329 in CinC). With the saturation, flatline and constant-metadata features removed, logistic regression reaches AUROC 0.746, but this subset was chosen after inspecting CinC and is exploratory.
4. **NSTDB degradation (paired design, seed 42): every learned model falls from +24 dB to +6 dB, and at 0 dB and −6 dB all but the 1D CNN are at or below 0.12.** Paired changes at +6 dB are −0.56 to −0.90 for the SQI models, the 2D CNN and the multimodal net. Several models rise again at −6 dB relative to 0 dB. The zero-shot d1-3B changes by at most 0.09 at any level and returns to 0.76 at −6 dB, so it does not track the noise. The heuristic value of 0.998 at clean control in the earlier draft could not be reproduced; the current implementation gives 0.740 on the 44 clean windows.
5. **Latency:** image-based pipelines spend about 92% of end-to-end time on rasterization (median over three runs: 2D CNN 60.2 ms of 65.2 ms); the 1D CNN ran in 1.47 ms median on MPS with cached inputs (1.36–1.49 ms across runs).
